Additional Services

Enhance Your Stay

Room Service

To help keep your room fresh and comfortable during longer stays, we offer housekeeping services based on the length of your stay.

Stays of 5 nights or more include one complimentary room cleaning service. Additional housekeeping services are available upon request for a nominal fee per service.

Stays of 3-4 nights can request housekeeping, charged per service. Our service includes room cleaning, fresh towels, and replenishment of essential amenities.

Local Experiences & Travel Assistance

Experience Fort Kochi like a local with personalized travel guidance and concierge services. Every guest receives a complimentary Fort Kochi map, and we're happy to share favorite attractions, hidden gems, local cafes, restaurants, cultural sites, and walking routes.

We can arrange trusted tuk-tuk tours around Fort Kochi or private taxi services to nearby destinations, with detailed itineraries, recommended package options, and transparent pricing.

We're also happy to assist with onward travel, including train schedules, local ferry and boat timings, bus information, and flight schedules.

At Guestland Homestay, we believe every journey should be effortless. From local recommendations to travel planning, we're here to help you experience Fort Kochi with confidence, comfort, and the warmth of genuine Kerala hospitality.
