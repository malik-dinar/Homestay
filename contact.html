Getting Here

How to Reach Us

✈️

By Air

The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK), located approximately 42 km from Guestland Homestay. Airport taxi transfers can be arranged on request.

Distance: 42 km
Travel Time: 1 hour 15 minutes approx.

🚂

By Train

The nearest railway stations are Ernakulam Junction (South) Railway Station and Ernakulam Town (North) Railway Station. We can assist with trusted taxi bookings from either station.

South Station: 13 km, 35-45 minutes
North Station: 15 km, 40-50 minutes

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in time is 12:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged based on availability. Please contact us in advance to request.

Do you offer airport pickup service?

Yes, we do have airport shuttle service. From the airport to our homestay it will take around 1 hour and it costs Rs.1900. Please provide your flight details at least 24 hours in advance like flight number, arrival time and terminal number so that our driver will be there with the placard with your name written on time.

What is your cancellation policy?

The guest can cancel free of charge until 2 days before arrival. The guest will be charged the total price of the reservation if they cancel in the 2 days before arrival. No-shows will be charged the full booking amount.

Are pets allowed at the homestay?

Pets are not permitted at Guestland Homestay to ensure a clean, comfortable, and allergy-friendly stay for all guests.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes! A complimentary full breakfast is included with all room bookings. We offer Kerala Style foods, and can accommodate dietary restrictions with advance notice.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept cash payments only in Indian Rupees (INR). Kindly ensure you have sufficient cash available at the time of check-in or check-out, as we do not accept credit cards, debit cards, digital payments, or bank transfers. If you have any questions regarding payment before your arrival, please feel free to contact us-we'll be happy to assist you.
